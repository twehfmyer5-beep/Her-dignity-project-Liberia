Our Vision & Mission

Guiding purpose behind Her Dignity Project - Liberia

Our Vision

To create a society where every girl can manage her menstrual health with dignity, confidence, and without barriers.

Our Mission

To empower girls and women through education, access to menstrual health resources, and community engagement.

Project Aim

  • Provide sanitary pads and hygiene materials
  • Educate young girls on menstrual health
  • Reduce stigma around menstruation
  • Support school attendance
  • Partner with organizations and donors

    • Key Activities

    • School outreach programs
    • Donation drives
    • Awareness campaigns
    • Community engagement
